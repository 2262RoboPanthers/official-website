Thank You to Our Sponsors   

Our team would not be possible without the generous support of our amazing sponsors. These organizations and individuals believe in the power of STEM education and the importance of inspiring the next generation of engineers, programmers, and innovators.
  From providing financial support to offering mentorship and resources, our sponsors play a crucial role in helping us design, build, and compete with our robots each season. We are grateful for their continued partnership and commitment to youth development through robotics.
